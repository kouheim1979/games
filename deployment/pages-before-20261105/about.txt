EVERYDAY JAPAN LAB

Practical systems from everyday Japan, examined for use elsewhere.

By Everyday Japan Lab · Reviewed 2026-09-25

What this site is for

Everyday Japan Lab is an independent early-stage publishing project. It explores household equipment, routines and services used or developed in Japan, then asks which problems they could solve in other places.

We start with a practical question: will something fit, what conditions does it need, what might it cost to operate, or how much should a household plan for? The answer can be that a system is unsuitable for a particular home.

What our work does and does not establish

The current guides are desk research using linked public and manufacturer sources. We have not physically tested the linked products and do not certify installations. We distinguish sales reports, official recommendations and observed adoption. None alone proves that a system is common everywhere or superior to alternatives.

Initial drafting and code development use AI assistance. Sources, assumptions and calculation methods are provided for inspection. The publisher remains responsible for checking the material and resolving corrections.

Independence

We have applied to Google AdSense to help support our free guides and tools. Paid relationships will be identified visibly and will not determine editorial rankings. We do not currently publish sponsored articles or paid affiliate links. Read the policy.
